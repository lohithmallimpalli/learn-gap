SYSTEM WORKFLOW

How LearnGap Works

A systematic diagnostic workflow designed to transform assessment mistakes into targeted conceptual mastery.

1

Take Assessment

Complete 10 focused multiple-choice questions across 5 essential sub-topics in your chosen subject.

2

Identify Gaps

Our grading engine computes your topic percentages and classifies areas as Strong (≥70%), Needs Improvement (50-69%), or Weak (<50%).

3

Learn with AI

Receive tailored AI study coaching, textbook chapters, video tutorials, and documentation matching your learning gaps.

4

Track Progress

Retake assessments and monitor your scores improving over time in your persistent longitudinal progress dashboard.
